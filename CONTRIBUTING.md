# Contributing

TraceLogica welcomes corrections and improvements to its public documentation.

## Before opening a change

- Keep examples free of real credentials, tenant identifiers, endpoints, and
  telemetry payloads.
- Do not publish internal architecture, infrastructure topology, operational
  procedures, private repository links, or unreleased vulnerabilities.
- Label proposed interfaces as proposals. Do not describe them as stable until a
  released version implements them.
- Prefer OpenTelemetry terminology and link to upstream specifications where
  appropriate.

## Style

- Use short sentences and descriptive headings.
- Define specialized terms on first use.
- Use `example.com` and obviously fictional identifiers in examples.
- Describe cryptography as quantum-resistant, not quantum-proof.

Security reports do not belong in public issues. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
